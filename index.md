# HML_LLC

Standard operating procedures: how the recurring things get done, written down
once so nobody has to hold it in their head.

## Status

The site is live and the content tree is [empty]{.gap}. This page is the whole
of it.

Procedures land here one at a time, as they get written. Not as a bulk import of
filed paperwork nobody has read since it was filed -- a page that arrives
without being re-read arrives already wrong, and this site has exactly one
reader who would be the person to discover that.

## What belongs here

Procedures, checklists, standards, and the paperwork trail described in the
abstract. How a thing is done, in a form somebody could follow without asking.

**Not** anything naming a person or a number: borrower names, balances, payoff
figures, account numbers, addresses tied to somebody. That lives in FileMaker
and it stays there.

!!! warning "This site is public, and no setting changes that"
    A GitHub Pages site is reachable by anyone even when its repository is
    private. An unlisted URL is obscurity, not access control, and a crawler
    does not need to be told where to look. The test before writing anything
    here is one question: **if a stranger read this page, would it matter?** If
    yes, it does not go on this site.

## Writing for this site

The authoring contract is shared across every site in this family and is
documented once, on the template site, which is a live example of every rule it
states:

- [The frontmatter contract](@template:frontmatter)
- [Writing a page: callouts, tabs, tables](@template:writing)
- [Links between pages and between sites](@template:links)

Those are **cross-site links**. They resolve through the peer's published page
index at build time rather than by anybody hardcoding a URL, which is why they
survive the other site reorganising itself.
